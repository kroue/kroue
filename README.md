## 🚀 Featured Work

### 📦 [InvenTrack](https://github.com/kroue/inventrack) — Inventory & Point-of-Sale System

> `Angular 22` · `TypeScript` · `Supabase` · `PostgreSQL` · `Tailwind CSS`

A complete inventory and POS platform for retail businesses. Cashiers ring up sales; owners see what is running out *before* it does.

- 🛒 POS checkout with cash and GCash payment recording
- 📉 Reorder-point forecasting from daily sales velocity, with automatic email alerts
- 🗓 Batch and expiry tracking with near-expiry risk scoring
- 📥 Full procurement flow: restock requests → purchase orders → deliveries → stock logs
- 📴 Offline-capable with background sync, plus Excel import/export
- 🔐 Role-based access (Admin / Cashier) enforced at the database level

---

### 🖨 [NVAGo](https://github.com/kroue/nva-go) — Printing Business Digitalization Platform

> `React Native` · `Expo` · `React` · `Supabase` · `PostgreSQL`

A dual-platform system that moved a traditional printing shop off pen-and-paper ordering.

- 📱 Customer mobile app: browse services, upload design files, chat with designers, track orders
- 💳 GCash proof-of-payment upload with staff-side validation
- 🖥 Web dashboard for staff: order queue, payment verification, status updates, reports
- 🧾 Downloadable receipts and full order history

---

### 📣 [Lantaw](https://github.com/kroue/lantaw-mobile) — Advertiser Feed Mobile App

> `React Native 0.74` · `Expo Router` · `TypeScript` · `NativeWind v4`

A mobile advertising platform with a companion [admin panel](https://github.com/kroue/lantaw-admin).

- 🔑 Onboarding and authentication flows
- 📰 Ad feed, ad creation, and in-app payment flow
- 🎁 Rewards system with wallet and analytics
- 🎨 Built on a custom design-token system for consistent branding
